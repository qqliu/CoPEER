So graph is three independent sets of size2, with all edges between different parts (complete 3-partite graph K_{2,2,2}). Maximal independent sets: you can take at most one vertex from each part? Actually within a part you can take both vertices because they are non-adjacent (they commute). So you could take both i and -i (they are non-adjacent). Also you could take vertices from other parts? If you take i and -i (both from same part) and also take j (from another part), then i and j are adjacent (non-commuting) => edge, so not independent.
