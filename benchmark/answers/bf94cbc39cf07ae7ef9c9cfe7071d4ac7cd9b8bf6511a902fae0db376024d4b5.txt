The minimum weight edge crossing that partition must be in T (by Cut Property). Could there be two distinct min-weight crossing edges? If there are two, say e and e', both with weight w. Since T is a spanning tree, T can contain at most one edge crossing the partition. Suppose T contains e. Then e' is not in T. But e' is min weight crossing. Could the MST be unique even though there's another min-weight edge e' not in T? Let's try to construct.
