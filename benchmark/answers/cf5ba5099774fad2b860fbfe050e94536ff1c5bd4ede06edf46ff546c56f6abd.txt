So for k=0 (t^0): need φ(a)φ(b)=4.
For k=1 (t^1): need φ(a)φ(b) = -4 (since multiply by -1 gives 4 => φ(a)φ(b) = -4).

Thus kernel consists of:
- pairs (a,b) with product 4 and k=0,
- pairs with product -4 and k=1.

Count pairs with product 4: we found 2 ( (1,1), (r^2,r^2) ).
Pairs with product -4: need φ(a)φ(b) = -4. This occurs when one is identity (φ=2) and other is r^2 (φ=-2) in either order. So (1, r^2) and (r^2,1). Also possibly when one is identity and other r^2? That's it. Also could be when both are something else giving -4? No, only those.

Thus number of such pairs = 2 (ordered pairs). Actually (1,r^2) and (r^2,1) are two.

Thus kernel size = (pairs with product 4)*(k=0) + (pairs with product -4)*(k=1) = 2*1 + 2*1 =4.
