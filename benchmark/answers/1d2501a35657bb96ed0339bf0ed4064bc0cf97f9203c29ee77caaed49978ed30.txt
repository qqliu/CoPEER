   But wait: τ_M is defined using the discrete-time embedded chain {X_n}_{n≥1}. However, the original process X_t is continuous-time. The occupation measure L_T involves continuous time. The hyper-exponential recurrence criterion in Wu's paper might be for discrete-time or skeleton processes. The paper might be applying it to the continuous-time process by considering the embedded jump chain or the skeleton at integer times. If the skeleton at integer times satisfies the recurrence, does that imply the continuous-time process satisfies it? Often, if the process has a density or is strong Feller, the recurrence for the skeleton implies recurrence for the continuous process. But I'm not entirely sure.
