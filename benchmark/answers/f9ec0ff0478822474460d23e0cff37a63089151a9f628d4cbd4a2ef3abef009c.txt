That might hold if all outside vertices have exactly two neighbors that are adjacent (not necessarily consecutive). But if all outside vertices have neighbors that are adjacent but not consecutive, then C might not extend.

So we need a cycle C that is not induced, such that there are chords connecting nonconsecutive vertices of C.
