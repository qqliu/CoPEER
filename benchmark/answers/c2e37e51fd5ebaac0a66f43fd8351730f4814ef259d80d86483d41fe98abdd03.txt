$\forall X \subseteq A : \map {\operatorname{id}_A} {\sup X} = \sup \set{\map{\operatorname{id}_A} x : x \in X}$

The dual of this statement would be:
$\forall X \subseteq A : \map {\operatorname{id}_A} {\inf X} = \inf \set{\map{\operatorname{id}_A} x : x \in X}$

This is a correct application of the duality principle. However, the paper doesn't apply the duality principle to the correct statement. Instead, it applies it to the incorrect statement in line 0019:
