What if we add more edges to create a situation where MST is unique but a cycle has two max weight edges equal? Let's try a graph with 5 vertices. Maybe we can have a cycle where the two max weight edges are not both in any MST because they are "blocked" by other edges. But the condition (2) applies to all cycles in G. If there is a cycle with two max weight edges equal, can the MST still be unique? I'm starting to suspect that it's impossible, and the theorem is true.
