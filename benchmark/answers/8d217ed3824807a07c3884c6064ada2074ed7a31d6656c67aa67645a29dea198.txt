Pair (A,C): A={1,2}, C={1}. Compute: A∩C={1} nonempty, A∩C^c={2} nonempty, A^c∩C=∅? A^c={3,4}∩{1}=∅, A^c∩C^c={3,4}∩{2,3,4}={3,4} nonempty. So intersections: nonempty: A∩C, A∩C^c, A^c∩C^c => three nonempty, one empty (A^c∩C). So not independent (need all four nonempty).

Pair (B,C): B={3,4}, C={1}. B∩C=∅, B∩C^c={3,4} nonempty, B^c∩C={1,2}∩{1}={1} nonempty, B^c∩C^c={1,2}∩{2,3,4}={2} nonempty. So three nonempty, one empty => not independent.

Thus F has no independent pair. So lemma holds.
