dH = ∏_{i=1}^N dH_{ii} ∏_{i<j} dH_{ij}

With the covariance:
⟨H_{ij}H_{kl}⟩ = δ_{il}δ_{jk} + δ_{ik}δ_{jl}

So:
⟨Tr(H^2)⟩ = ⟨∑_{i,j} H_{ij}H_{ji}⟩ = ⟨∑_{i,j} H_{ij}^2⟩

For the diagonal elements (i = j):
