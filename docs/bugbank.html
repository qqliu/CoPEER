The released objection dossiers

Each dossier connects a challenged source claim to a concrete counterexample and a Lean-checked certificate, and shows which human reviews are available for it, separately from its recorded automated checks. Use the filters below to browse all 284 released dossiers, the 147 human-approved core dossiers, or the 100 primary benchmark inputs.

Select a row or follow its record link to open the record below. Direct links have the form bugbank.html#record=<id>.

Populations, as the paper keeps them apart: the broad collection; the 284 screened dossiers in the public release, each with its source passage, counterexample and Lean certificate; the 147-dossier human-approved core from 137 distinct sources; and the 100 primary benchmark inputs from 92 problem clusters (131 scorable inputs with the shorter supplementary texts). Membership in the collection is not human approval. The nine nonapprovals of the final audit are excluded from the core. Third-party source passages keep their original attribution and terms; see the repository's third-party notices.
