# Third-party sources

The dossiers contain mathematical source passages and source references. Their
original attribution is preserved in the corresponding dossier and numbered
source file under `bugbank/`. The recovered primary-source document under
`bugbank/support/` retains its original authorship.

These source materials retain their respective authorship and applicable terms.
This repository does not apply a blanket license to third-party passages,
documents, or dependencies. Lean and Mathlib are obtained through the pinned
environments described in `bugbank/lean/`; their own license files accompany those
dependencies.

Submission-team and reviewer identifiers are removed from exported review
records. Authors of cited scientific works remain identified so that readers can
locate and attribute the original mathematics.
