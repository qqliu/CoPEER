# Terms of use

## No crawling for, and no training of, machine-learning models

The contents of this repository and of the website built from it (text, data, code, mathematical certificates and figures) are published so that people can read, check, reproduce and cite the work. They may **not** be used, in whole or in part, to train, fine-tune, evaluate for training purposes, or otherwise develop machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models, and they may not be collected or scraped for any such purpose. The authors expressly reserve their rights under Article 4(3) of Directive (EU) 2019/790 and any equivalent text-and-data-mining opt-out: text and data mining of this material is not permitted. Machine-readable policy declarations appear in `robots.txt` and `ai.txt`, and each page includes `noai` and `noimageai` directives.
