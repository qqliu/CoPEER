\section*{AI Use Statement}
Language models contributed to source triage, counterexample search, executable checkers, Lean development, semantic reviews, qualitative coding, experimental grading, mathematical follow-ups, and manuscript revision. Their contributions included mathematical reasoning and interpretation of source claims. The records identify which assessments were made by human reviewers and which by language models. We developed the analytic propositions in Appendix~\ref{app:metrics} with AI tools and give their proofs there. We are responsible for all claims, citations, and released evidence.

\section*{Ethics Statement}
Local objections are not blanket judgments about a source author's competence or the validity of an entire work. Source versions, prior corrections, known constructions, and limits of consequence are retained to avoid misleading discovery attribution. Reviewers' identities should remain deidentified in public evidence; source excerpts and redistributed artifacts require appropriate permissions and license compliance. This paper does not claim a representative estimate of source-platform error rates. Recorded human approvals apply to the artifact versions and review questions identified in each dossier; model-assisted judgments are distinguished from human assessments.
