\section{Related resources}
\label{sec:related}

Section~\ref{sec:discussion} discusses the related work. Table~\ref{tab:related} compares the resources closest to ours on two points: what they ask a system to do, and what evidence stands behind their reference answers. For Co-PEER's localization benchmark the reference evidence is the dossier itself, a concrete witness with a selected Lean proof, together with the recorded human review that defines the human-approved core from which the benchmark is drawn; the score evaluates the selected line.
