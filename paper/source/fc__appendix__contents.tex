\paragraph{Appendix guide.}
Section~\ref{sec:evidence} gives the essential definitions and source-correspondence requirements; the appendices group the supporting material by purpose, and each study keeps its own population, protocol, and numerical results.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt,topsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Examples and review.} Appendix~\ref{app:auditing-exhibits} works through source-linked counterexamples and what each establishes. Appendix~\ref{app:sqrt-proof} proves the square-root results, and Appendix~\ref{app:recent-human-review} documents the human reviews, the exclusions and replacement witnesses, and the human-approved core.
\item \textbf{Experiments.} Appendix~\ref{app:complementary-detail} gives the protocols and complete results of the search, repair, and retrieval studies. Appendix~\ref{app:cost-repro-procedures} provides the instruction study's prompts, grading rubrics, and token accounting. Appendix~\ref{app:certified-localization} details the localization benchmark's protocol and complete results, and Appendix~\ref{app:metrics} analyzes what the scores measure.
\item \textbf{Mathematical follow-ups.} Appendix~\ref{app:explanation-census} records the thirteen explanation packages. Appendix~\ref{app:structural-case-studies} states, documents, and proves the graph-coloring and rainbow-Schur results, and Appendix~\ref{app:research-extensions} covers the further certified families.
\item \textbf{Construction and context.} Appendix~\ref{sec:method} records the construction, audit, and executable-check procedures, and Appendix~\ref{sec:related} compares related resources.
\end{itemize}
