\subsection{Executable checks of the proposed witnesses}
\label{sec:results}




\label{sec:results-twins}

Across 604 executable tasks, the search produced 351 witnesses, 12 exhaustive searches without a witness in the specified finite set, 221 inconclusive searches, and 20 claims outside the permitted search setting. Separately written checkers confirmed 348 of the 351 proposed witnesses and rejected three because they did not violate the reconstructed condition, and all 955 search and checker programs passed exact-arithmetic inspection and deterministic replay with two hash seeds. A separate stage checked 16 selected algorithmic failures and confirmed all 16. These were chosen examples, so the result describes their checks rather than the fraction of all failures that the search would find. The repository supplies the outcomes and a count-replay command in \path{studies/executable_checks/README.md}, with program bindings and the 16 selected records in \path{studies/executable_checks/bindings/README.md}.
