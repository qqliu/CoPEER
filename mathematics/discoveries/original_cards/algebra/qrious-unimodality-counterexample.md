## Object and why it works

Set $a=(2,3,5,12)$ and $b=(1,1,1,4,6,9)$, both of total sum 22.
The Landau function $L(x)=\sum_i\lfloor a_i x\rfloor-
\sum_j\lfloor b_j x\rfloor$ is one-periodic. Partition $[0,1)$ at
every rational breakpoint $k/n$ for tuple entries $n$. On all 22 resulting
half-open intervals the checker verifies each floor is constant and the
sum is nonnegative. Periodicity proves the hypothesis for every real $x\ge0$.

For $[m]_q=1+q+\cdots+q^{m-1}$ and the associated factorials, exact
division gives
$D=\prod_i[a_i]_q!/\prod_j[b_j]_q!
=[10]_q[11]_q(1-q^2+q^4)$.
The complete ascending coefficient sequence of $(1+q)D$ is

```
1,3,4,4,5,7,9,11,13,15,16,15,14,15,16,15,13,11,9,7,5,4,4,3,1
```

If a proposed peak is at least 12, the nondecreasing prefix would force
$c_{10}\le c_{12}$; if it is below 12, the nonincreasing suffix would force
$c_{12}\ge c_{14}$. Both contradict the displayed valley $16,14,16$.
The checker verifies the original factorial identity, exact divisibility,
all-real Landau condition, and every possible peak.

## What it kills

For all positive integer tuples a,b satisfying sum floor(a_i*x)-sum floor(b_j*x)>=0 for every real x>=0, the polynomial (1+q) product [a_i]_q! / product [b_j]_q! is unimodal.

This is the statement in Conjecture 5 of the exact source version cited below,
with the source definitions retained in the vendored input.
