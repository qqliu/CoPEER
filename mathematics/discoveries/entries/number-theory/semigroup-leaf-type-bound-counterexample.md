# A genus 33 numerical semigroup leaf of type 16

> Deposition record dated 11 September 2026. Read [later mathematical and attribution updates](../../README.md#later-updates) alongside this original record.

## Object and why it works

Use $S=\{0\}\cup A\cup[50,\infty)$, where
$A=\{25,26,28,30,31\}\cup\{38,39,\ldots,48\}$.
Its positive elements are at least 25, so closure follows from the complete
tail starting at 50. Its gaps are $1,\ldots,24$ together with
$27,29,32,33,34,35,36,37,49$, giving $g=33$ and $F=49$.

Every member of $A$ is indecomposable. Every integer from 50 through 74
is a sum of two members of $S$, as enumerated by the checker; every
$n\ge75$ decomposes as $25+(n-25)$. Thus $A$ is exactly the set of minimal
generators, all at most $F$, and $S$ is a leaf.

The complete pseudo-Frobenius set is
$\{13,14,15,16,17,20,22,27,29,32,33,34,35,36,37,49\}$.
For a positive gap $x$, it suffices to test $s\in S$ with $0<s<50-x$;
larger sums lie in the complete tail. Negative candidates are excluded by
adding 25, except $x=-25$, where adding 26 suffices. The checker verifies
every candidate and these reductions. Hence $t=16$, whereas $33=4(8)+1$
would require $t\le2(8)-1=15$.

## What it kills

If S is a leaf of genus g>=2, write g=4q+r with 0<=r<4. Its type is at most 2q-1 for r=0 or 1, at most 2q for r=2, and at most 2q+1 for r=3.

This is the statement in Conjecture 4.4 of the exact source version cited below,
with the source definitions retained in the vendored input.

## Debugging uses

A conductor gives complete finite reductions; a bounded gap scan without the tail and negative-integer arguments would not certify type.
