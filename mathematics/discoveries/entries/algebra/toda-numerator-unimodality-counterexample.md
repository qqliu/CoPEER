# A normalized B3 Toda numerator with coefficient sequence 1,0,1

> Deposition record dated 11 September 2026. Read [later mathematical and attribution updates](../../README.md#later-updates) alongside this original record.

## Object and why it works

Use the root system $B_3=\{\pm e_i,\pm e_i\pm e_j:i<j\}$ with
$(x,y)=2x\cdot y$ and simple roots
$\alpha_1=e_1-e_2$, $\alpha_2=e_2-e_3$, $\alpha_3=e_3$.
The Gram matrix is $((4,-2,0),(-2,4,-2),(0,-2,2))$, and
$d_i=(\alpha_i,\alpha_i)/2$ gives $(2,2,1)$.
Set $a=(1,1,0)$. The checker verifies the finite root system,
crystallographic conditions, positive definiteness, and ambient normalization.

With $Q_a=\prod_i\prod_{j=1}^{a_i}(1-q^{d_i j})$, the source recursion is
$J_0=1$ and
$J_a=(1-q^{(\alpha,\alpha)/2})^{-1}
\sum_{0\le b\le a,b\ne a}q^{(\beta,\beta)/2}J_b/Q_{a-b}$.
Writing $f=1-q^2$ gives $J_{(1,0,0)}=J_{(0,1,0)}=f^{-2}$ and
$J_{(1,1,0)}=(1+q^2)/f^4$. Therefore $H_a=Q_a^2J_a=1+q^2$.
The exact checker enumerates all four recursive states and verifies
rational-function equalities by cross multiplication.

The full consecutive coefficient sequence is $(1,0,1)$, so no unimodal
peak exists. This conclusion uses ordinary powers of the source variable
$q$, including zero coefficients. Regrading with $t=q^2$ yields $1+t$,
which is unimodal and is a different claim. The ambient normalization
must be retained even though $a$ is supported on the two long simple roots.

## What it kills

For every finite reduced crystallographic root system normalized so short roots have squared length 2 and every nonnegative integer vector a, H_a(q)=(q)_a^2 J_a(q), defined by the source Toda recursion, is unimodal in consecutive ordinary powers of q.

This is the statement in Conjecture 7.3 of the exact source version cited below,
with the source definitions retained in the vendored input.

## Debugging uses

Keep ambient root normalization and zero coefficients when testing unimodality; do not silently regrade in q squared.
