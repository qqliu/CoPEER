# Six landmarks resolve the 36 vertices of F_2(K_8)

> Deposition record dated 11 September 2026. Read [later mathematical and attribution updates](../../README.md#later-updates) alongside this original record.

## Object and why it works

Vertices are nonnegative integer vectors of length $n$ with coordinate
sum $k$. Moving one token between distinct coordinates gives adjacency,
and $d(x,y)=\tfrac12\sum_i|x_i-y_i|$.
For $n=8,k=2$, choose the six landmarks
$e_1+e_2,e_1+e_3,e_1+e_4,e_5+e_6,e_5+e_7,e_5+e_8$.
There are $\binom{9}{2}=36$ vertices. The checker builds the graph from
token moves and independently checks all graph distances by breadth-first
search against the distance formula. The six-distance signatures of all
36 vertices are distinct, proving dimension at most 6 and refuting 7.

For an exact minimum, the checker additionally exhausts all
$\binom{36}{5}=376992$ five-landmark sets and finds an unresolved pair
for each. A smaller resolving set could be extended to five, so none
exists; the dimension is exactly 6. Supplementary checks at other values
of $n$ do not by themselves prove a general formula.

## What it kills

Conjecture 4.2: dim(F_k(K_n)) = n−1.

This is the statement in Conjecture 4.2 of the exact source version cited below,
with the source definitions retained in the vendored input.

## Debugging uses

A conjectured exact value can fail through a smaller explicit resolving set; the lower-bound enumeration is stronger than needed for refutation.
