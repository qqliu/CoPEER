=== REPLY / POST TEXT (verbatim, stored text) ===
0001 | Let $q = \dfrac a b$ with $a, b \in \Z$.
0002 | 
0003 | Let $\Z \sqbrk X$ denote the [[Definition:Ring of Polynomials|ring of polynomials]] over $\Z$.
0004 | 
0005 | Let $n \in \Z$ such that $n f \in \Z \sqbrk X$. 
0006 | 
0007 | Then we have:
0008 | :$b n \paren {q f} = a n f \in \Z \sqbrk X$
0009 | 
0010 | By the definition of [[Definition:Content of Rational Polynomial|content]], and using that $a \in \Z$:
0011 | :$\cont {b n q f} = a \cont {n f}$
0012 | 
0013 | By definition of [[Definition:Content of Rational Polynomial|content]]:
0014 | :$\cont {q f} = \dfrac 1 {b n} \cont {b n q f}$
0015 | 
0016 | Combining the above with the definition of $\cont f$:
0017 | :$\cont {q f} = \dfrac a b \dfrac 1 n \cont {n f} = q \cont f$
0018 | {{Qed}}
