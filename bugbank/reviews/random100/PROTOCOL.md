# Random audit: protocol and analysis chronology

This is an identity-free consolidation of the saved protocol and its three
pre-return addenda. It describes both the planned procedure and the analysis
used in the paper. Categorical judgments retain their original values.

## Population, sample and commitment

The population was the 284 retained dossiers: 191 in the main evaluation and 93
other dossiers. The withdrawn diagnostic was excluded. Draw 67 and 33 dossiers
by simple random sampling without replacement within these groups, respectively.
Use one pseudorandom generator: first draw a full permutation of sorted main
evaluation IDs, then a full permutation of sorted other IDs. Keep the first 67
and 33. `design.json` contains the frame and the numerical seed; `reproduce.py`
reconstructs the same ordered sample. The remaining permutation provides the
reserve order for an extension declared in advance. Overlap with the preceding
30-artifact review is permitted and does not alter selection.

The commitment was recorded internally before review and checked by another
collaborator after the draw. The commitment and draw were generated by the same
run less than one second apart. The seed included implementation-chosen text and
repository state. Thus the commitment is a hash-pinned internal record, not an
independently witnessed random beacon.

## Review material and questions

Packets presented the problem, challenged quotation, complete saved source with
line labels, counterexample, selected formal statements and their recorded proof
assumptions, exact Lean source, and saved build/environment evidence. Separate
prior verdict fields and interpretive commentary were omitted. Source and Lean
text were preserved, including any comments already in those files. Consequently
the reviewer could recognize material or encounter earlier construction notes.
The optional reference-line question followed the source and Lean evidence.

For each item, answer YES, NO, or CANNOT_DETERMINE with a reason:

1. Does the objection accurately represent the source claim and its assumptions?
2. Does the counterexample satisfy those assumptions and make the claim false?
3. Does the Lean statement correctly represent and verify that counterexample?

The optional fourth question classifies the consequence for the argument. The
optional fifth question asks whether the designated reference lines locate the
challenged claim; NOT_APPLICABLE is available when there are no reference lines.
Reviewers inspect the source and formal statement directly, check assumptions,
and treat comments as evidence to assess rather than supplied answers. Reviewers
report assistance, personal checking, prior exposure, relevant project
involvement, elapsed time, and the declarations actually examined.

The plan assigned one primary reviewer per item and a fixed 20-item overlap if
multiple reviewers participated. The received data used one human reviewer with
AI assistance, who personally checked all 100 judgments. No construction or
implementation involvement was reported. Prior project exposure was reported for
28 items and none for 72; all elapsed-time fields were blank. The public tables
retain these aggregate disclosures without reviewer identities.

## Prespecified analysis and subsequent clarification

The original protocol defined all-YES, any-NO, unresolved, absent, and ineligible
categories. Initial admissibility rules restricted project involvement and prior
exposure and used a receipt cutoff. The pre-return third addendum specified
exact hypergeometric inversion with inclusive tails and equality retained at
the cutoff. It changed the headline calculation to 97.5% components for each
group, combined by Bonferroni for at least 95% joint coverage. It retained 95%
per-group companion intervals, a separate unaided-human endpoint, and any-NO
counts. Denominators remained fixed at 67 and 33. Blank time fields were to be
flagged administratively, not used to discard judgments.

The first intake report categorized the returned rows using the original cutoff
and admissibility rules. After receipt, admissibility was clarified and all 100
returned judgments were included in the analysis reported in the paper. This
changed analysis inclusion, not mathematical answers. The weighted point estimate
was also added after receipt. The original protocol, initial intake, and later
analysis remain separate in the research record; this release supplies the
anonymous judgments and the final numerical calculation.

The final group totals are 63/67 and 28/33 all-YES. The joint interval concerns
sampling uncertainty under this AI-assisted, human-confirmed review procedure.
The separate unaided endpoint has zero successes because all rows report AI
assistance. `saved_analysis.json` preserves that endpoint together with the final
interval results. `exact_interval.py` contains the exact, unchanged interval
function extracted from the analyzer used in the final analysis; the saved
analysis binds its original source hash and individual function hashes.

## Follow-up on disagreements

Every NO answer is retained. `nine_flags.json` projects the scientific content
of the separate follow-up: five concerns had already been documented, one
involves a side condition, and three were newly recorded source-to-formal
representation concerns. These dispositions do not change the nine records'
original NO answers or increase the 91/100 approval count. They are follow-up
interpretations, not additional human votes or new Lean builds.
