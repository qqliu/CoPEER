## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
Let A = Set Unit (the subsets of a one-point set), ordered by inclusion, with arbitrary suprema given by union, and let id_A be the identity map. Take X = {∅} ⊆ A. The printed formula requires id_A(sup A) = sup{id_A(x) : x ∈ X}. Here sup A = univ (the whole one-point set) while sup{id_A(x) : x ∈ X} = sup X = ∅, and univ ≠ ∅. So the printed formula fails. The intended formula id_A(sup X) = sup{id_A(x) : x ∈ X} holds (both sides are sup X). The selected Lean theorem certifies idA (sSup univ) = univ, sSup (idA '' witnessX) = ∅ and their inequality, with idA = CompleteLatticeHom.id (Set Unit) and witnessX = {∅}.
~~~~
