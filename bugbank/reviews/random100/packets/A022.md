## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
Keep the path with vertices 4−5−6−7, M1={45,67}, and M2={56}. Both are maximal matchings: no further graph edge can be added to either. Their symmetric difference is the single alternating path of length 3, with two M1 edges and one M2 edge. Hence the asserted intermediate bound that these remaining M1 edges number at most |M2| fails: 2>1. The component is an odd alternating path, also refuting the source's proposed exhaustive component list. This does not refute the true final bound |M1|≤2|M2|. The original unbounded family remains valid: take k disjoint copies of this P4, for any k≥1, and use the same matchings in each component. Both unions are maximal matchings, since any addable edge would already be addable within one component. Their sizes are 2k and k, their intersection is empty, and the excess in the false intermediate inequality is k. This general counting argument, rather than the old three-value Lean check, supports the family.
~~~~
