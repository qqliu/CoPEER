4 | field at every input vertex is equal to (or smaller than) the length
5 | of the shortest edge in the input PLC.  There are PLCs for which the
6 | condition does not hold.  For instance, consider a line segment $ab$
7 | and a point $p$ close to it.  If no vertices other than $a$ and $b$
8 | are closer to $p$, the local sizing field at $p$ is equal to

11 | an advancing-front algorithm in such instances and then provide
12 | a suitable preprocessing algorithm to achieve our objective
13 | of placing vertices in an advancing-front manner.

15 | Let us assume that $\Delta abp$ is a poor-quality triangle.
16 | If we were to carry out Steiner vertex placement as in our
17 | previous algorithm, $\Delta abp$ would be split in an
18 | appropriate order depending on $|pa|$ and $|pb|$.
19 | Further, if the Steiner vertex for $\Delta abp$ encroaches
20 | upon segment $ab$, a new vertex $m$ would be added on $ab$,
21 | and all \emph{free} vertices in the circle/sphere with
22 | $m$ as the center and having a radius $|ma|$ (or $|mb|$) would

24 | even if it is inside the circle.  Thus, we would create
25 | a triangle with a smaller edge length $|mp|$.  This
