## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
Take G=K3 on {0,1,2}, so k+1=3 and k=2. Removing the single cycle edge12 gives GPrime={01,02}, a connected acyclic graph on the same three vertices. Then |E(GPrime)|=2=k rather than1=k-1. With m=1, |E(G)|=3=k+m rather than2=k-1+m. This refutes the local edge-count assertions in the attempted induction; it does not refute the theorem that a connected n-vertex graph with n-1 edges is a tree.
~~~~
