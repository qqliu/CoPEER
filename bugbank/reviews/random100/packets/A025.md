Title: Equivalence of Definitions of Transitive Closure of Relation/Union of Compositions is Smallest

Let $\RR$ be a [[Definition:Endorelation|relation]] on a [[Definition:Set|set]] $S$.

Let:
:$\RR^n := \begin {cases} \RR & : n = 0 \\ \RR^{n - 1} \circ \RR & : n > 0 \end {cases}$

where $\circ$ denotes [[Definition:Composition of Relations|composition of relations]].

Finally, let:
:$\ds \RR^+ = \bigcup_{i \mathop \in \N} \RR^i$

Then $\RR^+$ is the [[Definition:Smallest Set by Set Inclusion|smallest]] [[Definition:Transitive Relation|transitive relation]] on $S$ that [[Definition:Subset|contains]] $\RR$.
~~~~

## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
Let X={0,1,2}, R={(0,1),(1,2)}, and n=m=0. Both indices are natural. By the stated convention, R^(n+m)=R^0=R={(0,1),(1,2)}. But R^n∘R^m=R∘R={(0,2)}. Thus the two relations differ; for example, (0,1) belongs only to the left side.
~~~~
