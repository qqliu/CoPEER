## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
Take n₁ = 4 and p = 2. Then n₁ > 1, and 2 is prime and the largest prime factor of 4. The integers in {1,2,3,4} coprime to 4 are 1 and 3, so φ(4) = 2. Hence the largest prime factor of φ(4) is 2, equal to p rather than strictly less.
~~~~
