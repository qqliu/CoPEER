## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
Interpret ⊕ as pointwise addition. Let A = ZMod 2 and f,g,h : A →+ A be f=0, g=id, h=id. All are additive endomorphisms. At x=1: (g⊕h)(1)=1+1=0, so [f∘(g⊕h)](1)=0. But (f⊕h)(1)=1 and (f⊕g)(1)=1, so [(f⊕g)∘(f⊕h)](1)=1. Thus the maps differ.
~~~~
