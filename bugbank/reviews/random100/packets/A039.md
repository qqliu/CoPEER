## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
4-cycle; F={xv,yu}; H_A={xy} cuts G[A] but G−xy connected

Use the 4-cycle x-y-u-v-x. Let E be all four edges, i.e. delta({x,u}). Choose F={xv,yu}, a minimal cut; then G-F has components A={x,y} and B={u,v}. In G[A], HA={xy} is a minimal cut. But in the full graph G, deleting xy leaves the path x-v-u-y, so G-HA is connected. Thus HA is not even a cut in G.
~~~~
