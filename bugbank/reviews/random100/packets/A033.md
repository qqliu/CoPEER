9 | there exists a minimal element in this partially ordered set, which is a minimal
10 | edge cut contained in C.

12 | Proof. Suppose C1 ̸= C2 but C1 ∩C2 ̸= ∅. Then C1 ∩C2 would be a proper
13 | subset of both C1 and C2 that still separates the graph (since both C1 and C2
14 | separate the graph and they share edges). This contradicts the minimality of
15 | C1 and C2.
16 | Theorem 1. Every edge cut in G can be expressed as a disjoint union of min-
17 | imal edge cuts.
