## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
Take r=1/4 and s=1/2. Both are rational and r<s because 1/4<1/2. If s=1/n, then 1/2=1/n forces n=2. But then 1/(n+1)=1/3, which is not r=1/4. Thus this rational pair is not covered by the specialization.
~~~~
