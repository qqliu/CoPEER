## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
Let p=x and P=(x ∨ q), so p is a subformula of P. Replace p by p'=y, obtaining P'=(y ∨ q). Under the valuation x=true, y=false, q=false: P=true ∨ false=true, while P'=false ∨ false=false. Hence P and P' are not logically equivalent.
~~~~
