## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
Take epsilon = 2/5, N = 3 and n = 4. Then epsilon > 0, 1/N = 1/3 < 2/5, and N is the least integer at least 1/epsilon = 5/2; also n >= N. Nevertheless, sqrt(n) = 2 gives 4/(sqrt(n)+2) = 1, while 1/n = 1/4. Hence the displayed strict inequality is false even under the surrounding choices in the source.
~~~~
