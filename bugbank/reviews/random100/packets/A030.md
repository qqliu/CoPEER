## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
Over Q (or R), take A=[[2,2],[2,2]]. Every entry is prime, so each of its two rows and two columns contains exactly two prime-valued entries. A is nonzero, but its two rows are identical; equivalently det(A)=2·2−2·2=0. Hence rank(A)=1, contradicting the claimed rank ≥2.
~~~~
