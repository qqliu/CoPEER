## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
Take real a=0 and x=1. There are no stated hypotheses excluding this choice. Then |a|=0, so the proposed expression sec(theta)=x/|a| requires the ordinary-real quotient 1/0 and is undefined. Equivalently, the reverse parametrization x=|a|sec(theta) gives x=0 for every theta where sec(theta) is defined, so it cannot represent x=1.
~~~~
