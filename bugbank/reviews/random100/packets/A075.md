## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
Let A(n) execute exactly 2^n iterations. Schedule one iteration every 18 months. Then its wall-clock runtime is T(n)=18·2^n months, not 18n. For example, T(4)=288 months and T(5)=576 months. More generally T(n)/n=18·2^n/n is unbounded, so T is not O(n) and hence not linear.
~~~~
