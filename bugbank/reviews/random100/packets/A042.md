## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
Let f(n)=2n. Define g(1)=2 and g(n)=n for n≠1. Then g is not injective because g(1)=g(2)=2 with 1≠2. Since 2n≠1 for every n∈N, (g∘f)(n)=g(2n)=2n. Thus (g∘f)(a)=(g∘f)(b) implies 2a=2b, hence a=b.
~~~~
