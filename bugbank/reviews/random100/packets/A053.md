## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
Let A={0}, so P(A)={∅,{0}}. Define f:P(A)→P(A) by f(∅)=∅ and f({0})={0}. For each Y∈P(A), choose X=Y; then f(X)=Y. Thus f is surjective.
~~~~
