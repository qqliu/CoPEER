## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
Take 500/500 and 9/9. Their denominators are exactly 500 and 9, respectively. Both equal 1; equivalently, cross-multiplication gives 500·9 = 9·500 = 4500. Thus these fractions are equivalent, refuting the universal claim.
~~~~
