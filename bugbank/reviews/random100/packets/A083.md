## 4. Exact released witness

~~~~text
Take the prime p=11. Then N=2^(11-1)(2^11-1)=2^10·2047=2^10·23·89=2,096,128. The sum of all positive divisors is (1+2+⋯+2^10)(1+23)(1+89)=4,421,520, whereas 2N=4,192,256. Thus the proper-divisor sum is 2,325,392≠N, so N is not perfect.
~~~~
