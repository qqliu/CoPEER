## 1. Stored dossier title/problem

~~~~text
Title: Characterization of Natural Transformation Between Bifunctors

Let $\mathbf C, \mathbf D$ and $\mathbf E$ be [[Definition:Category|categories]].

Let $F, G : \mathbf C \times \mathbf D \to \mathbf E$ be [[Definition:Bifunctor|bifunctors]] from the [[Definition:Product Category|product category]] $\mathbf C \times \mathbf D$ to the [[Definition:Category|category]] $\mathbf E$.

For each [[Definition:Object (Category Theory)|object]] $C \in\mathbf C$ let: 
:$F \tuple{C, -}$ and $G \tuple{C, -}$
denote the [[Definition:Functor|functors]] obtained by [[Definition:Bifunctor|substituting the first variable]] of $F$ and $G$, respectively, with $C$.

For each [[Definition:Object (Category Theory)|object]] $D \in\mathbf D$ let:
:$F \tuple{-, D}$ and $G \tuple{-, D}$
denote the [[Definition:Functor|functors]] obtained by [[Definition:Bifunctor|substituting the second variable]] of $F$ and $G$, respectively, with $D$.

For each [[Definition:Object (Category Theory)|object]] $\tuple{C, D} \in \mathbf C \times \mathbf D$ let:
:$\alpha_{\tuple{C, D}} : F \tuple{C,D} \to G \tuple{C,D}$ 
denote a [[Definition:Morphism (Category Theory)|morphism]] in $\mathbf E$.

Then:
:$\alpha : F \to G$ is a [[Definition:Natural Transformation|natural transformation]]
{{iff}}
:$(1)\quad$ For each [[Definition:Object (Category Theory)|object]]  $C \in \mathbf C : \alpha \tuple{C, -} : F \tuple{C, -} \to G \tuple{C, -}$ is a [[Definition:Natural Transformation|natural transformation]]
:$(2)\quad$ For each [[Definition:Object (Category Theory)|object]]  $D \in \mathbf D : \alpha \tuple{-, D} : F \tuple{-, D} \to G \tuple{-, D}$ is a [[Definition:Natural Transformation|natural transformation]]

where:
:for [[Definition:Object (Category Theory)|objects]] $C \in \mathbf C$ and $D \in \mathbf D$, $\alpha \tuple{C, -}_D = \alpha_{\tuple{C, D}}$
and:
:for [[Definition:Object (Category Theory)|objects]] $C \in \mathbf C$ and $D \in \mathbf D$, $\alpha \tuple{-, D}_C = \alpha_{\tuple{C, D}}$
~~~~
