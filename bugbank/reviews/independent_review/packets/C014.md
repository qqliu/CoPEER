45 | The notation for the type I elements looks more cumbersome than the underlying concept.  The notation is motivated by the zigzagging shape of the corresponding heaps.  The index $i$ tells us where to start and the index $j$ tells us where to stop.  The L (respectively, R) tells us to start zigzagging to the left (respectively, right).
