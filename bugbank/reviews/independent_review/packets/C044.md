40 | There are many unsolved problems in Number Theory -- most of which can be stated in an extremely simple form -- that have presented a serious challenge to mathematicians for many years. Some considerable progress has been made lately, and it is the purpose of this article (and three papers to follow) to report the advances made.\\

42 | In this article, we shall prove two ``famous'' hypotheses, among other things. The first is Andrica's conjecture (Theorem \ref{Th3}), simultaneously proposed by Andrica \cite{Andrica} and the current author \cite{Grandpa} in 1986. Andrica's conjecture has been proved in the limit as $k\rightarrow \infty$ before \cite{Grandpa, Wolf}, but this is the first time it is proved for all consecutive primes. The second such result is the proof of Legendre's conjecture (Theorem \ref{Th5}), one of Landau's problems -- so far unsolved \cite{Landau} despite numerous  attempts. We present two different ways of proving it.
