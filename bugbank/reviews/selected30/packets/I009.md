## 4. Supplied witness

~~~~text
isolated added vertices

Take the source graph G to have one vertex v and no edges, so n=1. Then G′ has v plus 17 added isolated vertices. Under the standard convention that a one-vertex sequence is a simple path, the path [a1] consisting of one added isolated vertex visits exactly n=1 vertices, contradicting the local claim that no added isolated vertex can be on such a path. This is a local edge-case witness, not a refutation of the NP-hardness statement.
~~~~
