## 4. Supplied witness

~~~~text
Let C2={0<1} and C3={0<a<1}, both finite frames. Define h:C2→C3 by h(0)=0 and h(1)=1, and f=h^op. The RHS holds explicitly. h preserves bottom, top, all finite meets, and arbitrary joins (check the four subsets of C2). But h is not surjective since a is not in its image, hence h is not a frame isomorphism and f is not a locale isomorphism. Thus RHS=true while LHS=false.
~~~~
